Learn in 2 minutes

SIP (monthly investing)

Invest a fixed amount every month in a broad index fund. You buy more units when prices are low and fewer when high. It needs no timing and has the longest track record of the ideas here.

200-day average (trend check)

If the index is above its 200-day average the long-term trend is up. Below it, the trend is weak. Many long-term investors keep their SIP going but add lump sums in small parts when the trend is weak.

Momentum (monthly picks)

Stocks that have been strongest over the last 6 months have tended to keep outperforming for a while, in many markets. Each month you hold the 10 strongest in equal parts, and stay in cash if the market trend is down.

Swing trade

Holding a stock for days to weeks. Classic rules: buy a dip to the 20/50-day average in an uptrend, or a break above the 20-day high. Always set the stop first.

Stop (get out below)

The price where you leave if you were wrong. It keeps a loss small and planned.

Targets (TP1, TP2, TP3)

Levels where you take profit: 1.5, 2 and 3 times your risk. Selling part at each level locks in gains. A close target is hit more often but pays less.

Position size

Risk about 1% of your money per idea. On Rs 1 lakh that is Rs 1,000, so shares = Rs 1,000 divided by the distance to your stop.

Spread your money

Do not hold only banks, or only one idea. Several stocks falling together is the main risk. Taxes and costs also apply, so check current rules before investing.

Simple routine

  1. Keep the monthly SIP running.
  2. Once a month, review the momentum list.
  3. For swing ideas, choose at most 3, set the stop first.
  4. On Sunday, check the journal and the live test.
