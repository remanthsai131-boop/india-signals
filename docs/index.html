5. Learn in 2 minutes

Buy range

The price area where an idea makes sense. If the price is above it, you would be chasing, so wait.

Stop (get out below)

The price where you leave if you were wrong. Decide it before you buy, so a loss stays small and planned.

Sell range

Where you take profit. The first level is twice your risk and the second is three times.

Risk per trade

Never risk more than 1% of your money on one idea. On Rs 1 lakh, that is Rs 1,000.

R

One R is what you risk on a trade. Winning 2R means winning twice what you risked.

Practice (paper) trading

Trade with pretend money until your record proves the idea works. Real money only after that.

Why most traders lose

Costs, emotions and random noise. Even good ideas lose about half the time, so winners must be bigger than losers.

Trend

Price above its 50 and 200 day averages means an uptrend. Buy dips in uptrends and avoid falling markets.
